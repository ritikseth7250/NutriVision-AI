How NutriVision AI Operates

A streamlined, three-stage pipeline connecting visual meal recognition to clinical dietary analytics.

1. Image Acquisition

Capture via mobile camera or upload dish images with drag-and-drop support. Normalizes orientation, resolution, and exposure for deep network evaluation.

2. AI Model API Call

Sends the uploaded meal photo to an AI vision model via API. Automatically detects dishes, segments multi-item plates, and extracts ingredients in real time.

3. Nutrition & Assessment

Estimates caloric load and macro composition (Protein, Carbs, Fat, Fiber), followed by personalized guidance to assist informed eating habits.
